Privacy policy

Mawkeb Quotation is used by staff of Al Mawkeb Technologies L.L.C. to prepare quotations for customers. This policy explains what the app stores and why.

What we store

  • Your account: your name, mobile number and email address, and a password (the password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication and is never visible to us).
  • Quotations: the quotations you make – customer name, contact details you type in, items, prices and terms – and who made or changed each quotation and when.
  • Activity: a log of actions in the app (for example “created quotation”, “sent on WhatsApp”, “followed up”) so the company admin can see the work done.

Why – only to run the app: to log you in, to let approved staff share the company’s quotations, and to let the admin approve accounts and follow the work.

Where – the data is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, Doha region) and in a copy on the phones and computers of approved staff. Quotation PDFs are created on your device and are only shared when you choose to send them.

Who can see it – only staff accounts approved by Al Mawkeb Technologies. Your name and mobile are visible to the company admins. We do not sell data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics.

Customer details – customer names and contact details are entered by staff to prepare and send quotations, and are used only for that.

Deleting your account – in the app, open Settings → Account → Delete my account. Your login, name and mobile are deleted immediately. Quotations you prepared remain part of the company’s business records. You can also ask for deletion using the support contact above.

Changes – if this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page.
